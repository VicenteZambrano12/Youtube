Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

This app requests only the TikTok permissions user.info.basic and video.publish.

  • Data accessed: basic profile info (open ID, display name, avatar) and OAuth tokens issued by TikTok.
  • Use: solely to publish videos the account owner selects to their own TikTok account.
  • Storage: tokens are stored locally on the operator's computer and are never sold or shared with third parties.
  • Deletion: you can revoke access at any time in TikTok → Settings → Security → Apps and services; local tokens are then deleted.
